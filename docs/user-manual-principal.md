## 1. Getting Started

Quanby Legal provides electronic notarization - a digital version of traditional notarization with the same legal validity. Sign documents from anywhere in the Philippines or at Philippine embassies abroad through secure video technology (REN) or in-person with digital tools (IEN). The platform offers faster processing, tamper-evident documents, digital storage, and access to session recordings.

**System Requirements**: Use Google Chrome (recommended), Mozilla Firefox, Safari, or Edge on a computer or mobile device with camera and microphone. Maintain stable internet connection (minimum 2 Mbps). Have a valid government-issued ID ready (Philippine ID, Passport, Driver's License, PRC ID, or other valid ID). Prepare your documents in PDF format.

**Registration**: Go to the registration page and enter your full legal name (must match your ID), email address, and phone number. Create a secure password, accept Terms of Service, and click "Create Account." Check your email for the verification link and click it to activate your account.

**KYC Verification**: Complete Know Your Customer verification - a one-time identity verification required by BSP regulations before using the platform. Upload your government-issued ID (front and back if applicable), take a selfie photo, and the system verifies your face matches your ID photo. Verification is usually instant, after which you receive "KYC Verified" status.

## 2. Finding an ENP

**Browse & Select**: View all available Electronic Notary Publics (ENPs) with filters for specialization, ratings, location, and language. Read reviews, check badges, and select specific date and time. Use this when you know exactly what you need, want to choose a specific notary, or prefer someone with specific experience. It offers flexibility with no immediate time limit.

**Quick Match**: System finds the best available ENP for you based on a fair scoring algorithm. This is the fastest way to get started. You can accept or find another ENP up to 2 times, then there's a 10-minute cooldown. Use Quick Match when you need to book quickly and don't have a specific ENP preference.

**Message First**: Chat with ENPs before booking to ask questions about your documents, discuss requirements, and get to know them. Upgrade to a session booking when ready. Use this when you have complex documents, need advice before notarization, or want to establish rapport with an ENP.

**Choosing an ENP**: For Browse & Select, go to `/browse` → "Browse ENPs" tab, use filters for specialization, rating, location, and language, then click on an ENP profile. Review their qualifications, credentials, client reviews, ratings, badges, fees, and specializations. Check their availability calendar, select a date and time slot, choose session type (Consultation/Notarization), choose mode (REN/IEN/Hybrid), and submit booking request. Wait for ENP to accept.

## 3. Booking & Preparation

**Session Types**: Consultations are for asking questions, getting legal advice, or reviewing documents with no official documents produced - just advice. Duration is typically 30 minutes, payment is upfront, and you can upgrade to notarization if needed. Notarization sessions officially notarize documents with notarized documents (PDF) and entry in notarial book as output. Duration varies by document count, all parties must be present (principal, witnesses, ENP), and payment is upfront.

**Session Modes**: REN (Remote) means all parties join via video call from their own locations - everyone must be in the Philippines or at Philippine embassy/consular office. IEN (In-Person) means all parties are physically present with the ENP at their office or designated location. Hybrid combines both approaches, useful for situations like OFWs at embassy plus family in the Philippines.

**Document Upload**: You or the ENP can upload documents in PDF format. If you upload, the ENP reviews, sets the fee, and may request changes. If the ENP uploads, you review, approve, or request changes. All documents must be locked before the session starts. Prepare your documents by ensuring they're complete, accurate, and ready for notarization.

**Inviting Witnesses**: From session details, click "Add Witness," enter their name, email, and optional phone number, select their participation mode (Remote or In-person), and send. Witnesses receive email with instructions. They must register and complete KYC before the session. There's no limit on the number of witnesses, and no separate fee is charged - it's covered by your payment.

**Understanding Fees**: Document fees are set by each ENP and vary by document type (e.g., Acknowledgment ₱500). Additional charges include ₱50 per extra page and ₱100 per extra signatory. A 10% platform fee is deducted automatically. The payment split is 90% to ENP, 10% to platform. For IEN sessions, you can pay cash directly to the ENP who clicks "Client Paid Cash" to confirm payment. Payment is required before the session starts to enable the "Join Session" button.

## 4. Your Session

**Pre-Session Checklist**: Ensure stable internet connection (2 Mbps minimum), working camera and microphone, good lighting on your face, valid government ID ready, all documents reviewed and locked, payment completed, and all witnesses registered and KYC verified. Security checks include liveness check proving you're present now, geolocation check confirming you're in the Philippines or at embassy, and VPN detection and blocking. After all checks pass, the "Join Session" button enables and you can enter the video call.

**REN Session Flow**: All parties join the video call, system verifies geolocation of all participants, each participant completes liveness check one by one, ENP presents documents one at a time, you review and sign each document, all witnesses sign each document, ENP affixes notarial seal, and repeat for all documents. Session completes and documents are distributed to all parties.

**Liveness Check**: This real-time face verification proves you're a real human present now, preventing fraud from pre-recorded videos, photos, or masks. Follow on-screen instructions (blink, turn head, smile) taking 30-60 seconds per person. Every participant must pass before the session begins. Ensure you're in a well-lit area with a plain background, face the camera directly, and remove hats, glasses, or face coverings.

**Signing Process**: ENP presents Document 1, you sign first as Principal, all witnesses sign, ENP affixes notarial seal last, Document 1 completes, ENP presents Document 2, and repeat for all documents. Click the "Sign" button, use your electronic signature, confirm each signature, wait for all parties to complete, and the ENP signs last with notarial seal. Documents are signed in locked order without skipping.

## 5. After Your Session

**Receiving Documents**: You'll receive notarized documents (PDF with digital seal) and receipts/proof of notarization via email. Access them anytime at `/documents` in your account to download PDF copies. Documents are digitally signed by all parties, contain the ENP's electronic notarial seal, are tamper-evident (any changes invalidate the seal), and serve as legal proof of notarization.

**Session Recordings**: Principals can create and download recordings if enabled. Recordings are stored securely per Supreme Court requirements and accessible from `/sessions` or email links. They show video of all participants and the signing process throughout the retention period.

**Leaving Reviews**: Rate the ENP on professionalism, timeliness, communication, and satisfaction using a 1-5 star scale. Your review helps other clients choose the right ENP. ENPs also rate you, which affects your Reliability Score. Be fair and specific in your feedback.

**Verifying Documents**: Use the platform's verification tool to check document authenticity. Enter the document control number, and the system confirms authenticity and integrity. This proves your document is legitimate and hasn't been tampered with.

## 6. Security & Privacy

**Liveness Detection**: Confirms you're a real person present now, preventing fraud from photos, videos, or masks. Required before every session using anti-spoofing technology that detects pre-recorded videos and photo attacks.

**Geolocation**: Verifies your physical location, confirming you're in the Philippines or at an embassy. VPN usage is blocked. Principals and witnesses must be in the Philippines or at a Philippine embassy/consular office. ENPs are restricted from performing acts outside the Philippines.

**Data Privacy**: The platform is compliant with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and registered with the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Information is shared only with the Supreme Court per e-Notarization Data Sharing Guidelines. All communications use end-to-end encryption. The data privacy notice explains collection and SC data sharing requirements.

**Data Collection**: From principals, we collect name, contact information, ID documents for KYC, biometric data for liveness verification, and session recordings if enabled. From witnesses, we collect the same information. All data is encrypted and stored securely.

## 7. FAQ

**How long does notarization take?** Typical sessions last 30-60 minutes depending on the number of documents and signatories. Simple documents with few parties can be faster, while complex documents with multiple parties and witnesses take longer.

**Can I reschedule?** Yes, contact your ENP through messages to request rescheduling. The ENP can accept, decline, or suggest a new time. Be courteous and provide as much notice as possible.

**What if my internet disconnects?** Reconnect as soon as possible. The ENP may pause the session to wait for you. If you experience frequent connection issues, consider rescheduling to an IEN (in-person) session instead.

**How do witnesses join?** You send invitations from the session details page. Witnesses receive an email with instructions to register and complete KYC verification. They join the video call at the scheduled session time.

**Can I add documents after booking?** Yes, you can add documents, but only before the ENP locks all documents. Once documents are locked, no new documents can be added to the session. Upload additional documents early if you think they might be needed.

**What file formats are accepted?** PDF is required for documents. JPG or PNG formats are accepted for ID uploads during KYC. If you have document images, convert them to PDF before uploading.

**Is my payment refundable if I cancel?** Refund policies depend on the ENP's cancellation policy, which you should check on their profile before booking. Contact the ENP directly to discuss cancellation and potential refunds.

**How do I verify a notarized document is authentic?** Use the platform's verification tool and enter the document control number. The system will confirm the document's authenticity and integrity, proving it's legitimate and hasn't been tampered with.

## 8. Tips & Troubleshooting

**Best Practices - Before Session**: Complete KYC well in advance to avoid delays. Upload documents early so the ENP has time to review them. Test your camera and microphone before the session. Have your physical ID ready and visible. Ensure good lighting on your face. Check your internet speed (2 Mbps minimum recommended). Invite witnesses early so they can complete KYC. Pay before the session starts to enable the "Join Session" button.

**Best Practices - During Session**: Join 5-10 minutes early to test connection. Have your ID visible and ready. Follow the ENP's instructions carefully. Speak clearly and professionally. Review documents thoroughly before signing them. Ask questions if you don't understand anything. Stay until the session completes and all documents are notarized.

**Best Practices - After Session**: Download and save your notarized documents immediately. Save receipts and proofs of notarization. Access recordings if enabled for your records. Leave a thoughtful review of the ENP. Check all documents for accuracy. Store documents securely in a safe location. Never share recordings publicly, and never alter notarized documents as this invalidates the seal.

**Common Issues - KYC Verification Fails**: Check that your ID is valid and not expired. Ensure your selfie clearly shows your face. Make sure lighting is good and use a plain background. If verification fails multiple times, try a different ID or contact support.

**Common Issues - Can't Join Session**: Verify all documents are locked. Confirm payment is complete. Check that all witnesses have completed KYC. Ensure your geolocation check passes (you must be in the Philippines or at embassy). Make sure the ENP hasn't cancelled the session.

**Common Issues - Liveness Check Fails**: Ensure you're in a well-lit area. Remove hats, glasses, or face coverings. Face the camera directly with your full face visible. Follow the prompts slowly and clearly. Try multiple times before giving up.

**Common Issues - Payment Problems**: Check your payment method is valid. Verify your card has sufficient funds. Contact your bank if a transaction is declined. For IEN sessions, ask the ENP if they can accept cash payment instead.

**Common Issues - Internet Connection**: Check your speed (2 Mbps minimum recommended). Use a wired connection if possible instead of WiFi. Close other applications using bandwidth. The ENP may reschedule if your connection is too poor for a successful session.

**Support Channels**: Use the Help Center for FAQs and guides. Access Live Chat for real-time support during business hours (Monday to Friday, 8 AM - 5 PM PHT). Email Support with questions, typically responding within 24 hours. Contact your ENP directly for session-specific issues.
