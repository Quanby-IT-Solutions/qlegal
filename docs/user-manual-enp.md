# Quanby Legal - User Manual for Electronic Notary Publics (ENPs)

**Version**: 1.0  
**Last Updated**: January 2026  
**Target Audience**: Electronic Notary Publics (ENPs)

## 1. Quick Start

Quanby Legal is your Electronic Notarial Facility (ENF) - a secure, SC-compliant digital platform for electronic notarization. As an ENP, you'll conduct REN (Remote) and IEN (In-Person) notarizations, automate your notarial book entries, and expand your client base beyond geographic limitations. The platform maintains the same legal authority as traditional notarization while providing secure digital tools.

**System Requirements**: Use Google Chrome on a computer or tablet with camera and microphone. Maintain stable internet connection (minimum 2 Mbps). Have your valid notarial commission from the Supreme Court, professional license information, and digital signature setup ready. For IEN sessions, prepare a dedicated physical space with proper lighting and a tablet/device for signing.

**Accreditation Process**: First, apply for your electronic notarial commission through the Supreme Court. Complete the SC-mandated instructional materials and receive your training completion certification. Submit your credentials to the SC for verification and wait for accreditation approval. Once approved, you'll receive ENF credentials and can register on Quanby Legal.

## 2. Registration & Setup

**Registration**: Go to the ENP registration page and enter your professional details including full name, notarial commission number, expiration date, PRC license (if applicable), and contact information. Upload your commission certificate, set up your digital signature and notarial seal, create account credentials, accept the Terms of Service and ENP agreement, then click "Register." Verification typically takes 24-48 hours, after which you'll receive an approval notification.

**Profile Setup**: Add a professional photo and list your specializations to help clients find you. Set your consultation rate (per 30 minutes) and notarization fees by document type - you can use platform defaults or customize based on your market. Configure your availability calendar with the days and hours you're accepting bookings. Set your Quick Match preferences including maximum sessions per day, session types you accept (notarization and/or consultation), and modes (REN, IEN, Hybrid).

**Dashboard Overview**: Your dashboard shows today's upcoming sessions, incoming booking requests, quick actions for managing sessions and viewing your notarial book, recent activity, and notifications. Key navigation includes `/dashboard` for your main overview, `/requests` for incoming bookings, `/sessions` for all past and upcoming sessions, `/notarial-book` for official records, `/messages` for client communication, and `/profile` for account management.

## 3. Managing Booking Requests

**Request Types**: Browse & Select requests come from clients who specifically chose you after reviewing your profile. These show client preferences and document details with no immediate time limit, giving you flexibility to accept, decline, or suggest new times. Quick Match requests are system-matched based on our algorithm - you have 60 seconds to accept, and declining affects your ENP score. Message-initiated requests come from clients you've chatted with first, offering more flexibility in responding.

**Handling Requests**: When you receive a notification (email and in-app), click to view request details including client name and reliability score, service type, session mode, documents uploaded, and requested date/time. Review the client profile and ratings, then choose your action: Accept to confirm the booking, Decline to reject (optionally with a reason), or Suggest New Time to propose alternatives. The client receives your response immediately.

**Quick Match System**: The smart matching algorithm pairs clients with the best available ENP using a bidirectional scoring system. You can opt-in or opt-out anytime with no penalty. Quick Match notifications show client name, photo, verification status, reliability score, session history, badges, service type, mode, and preferred time. Accept within 60 seconds to confirm and add the session to your calendar. Declining affects your score by 5-10% for excessive declines. Better to opt-out if you're unavailable.

**Time Limits**: Browse & Select requests have no immediate time limit, giving you flexibility to review and respond. Quick Match requires a 60-second response window - if you don't respond, it auto-declines and counts against your score. Message-initiated requests offer flexible response times since you've already established communication with the client.

## 4. Preparing Sessions

**Document Review Process**: Clients may upload documents for you to review, or you can upload documents yourself. When reviewing client uploads, verify content accuracy, check for required notarial clauses, ensure completeness, add or correct the document type tag, set your notarization fee, then Approve, Request Changes, or Reject. When uploading your own documents, select the PDF file, add a document type tag, set the notarization fee, upload, and wait for client review and approval.

**Document Locking**: Once all documents are reviewed (by either you or the client), each has a type tag and fee set, and the client has approved your uploaded documents, click "Lock All Documents." Quanby uses a full document-freeze lock contract: upload/add, reorder, signer edits, and project creation are blocked while locked, while signing progression is still allowed in the locked order. See [Meeting Document Lock Contract](lock-contract.md) for the authoritative rules.

**Pre-Session Checklist**: Verify all documents are reviewed and locked, client has completed KYC, all witnesses are registered and KYC verified, and payment is confirmed. For all sessions, ensure your camera and microphone are working, you have good lighting, and your internet connection is stable (minimum 2 Mbps). For IEN sessions, prepare your physical space for in-person parties and have your tablet/device ready. For REN sessions, ensure recording is enabled if applicable and video call software is tested.

## 5. Conducting Sessions

**Session Flow**: For REN sessions, all parties join via video call from their locations (must be in the Philippines or at Philippine embassy/consular office). For IEN sessions, all parties are physically present at your office or designated location. Hybrid sessions combine both approaches. Start by verifying all participants are present and their identities match their KYC records.

**Liveness Verification**: Have all parties join the video call, then conduct liveness checks one by one. Click on each participant, the system prompts for liveness actions (blink, turn head, smile), the system verifies they are live, and the check passes. All participants must pass before signing begins. If a check fails, ask the participant to improve lighting, remove face coverings, face the camera directly, and allow them to retry. Consider rescheduling if issues persist.

**Guiding Principals and Witnesses**: Verify each participant's identity matches their KYC and check government IDs. Present documents one at a time in the locked order, sharing your screen so all can see clearly, and explain content if needed. Instruct participants when to sign, ensure everyone completes their signature, and verify each signature is affixed. Sign last (after principal and witnesses) to affix your electronic notarial seal, adding the notarial certificate if applicable. Repeat for all documents in order.

**Affixing Notarial Seal**: After all principals and witnesses sign a document, review it one last time, then click "Sign and Affix Notarial Seal." Your electronic signature and seal are automatically applied, containing your full name, notarial commission number, date and time of notarization, location, and unique document control number. The document is marked as notarized and you move to the next document. The seal is tamper-evident - any changes invalidate it.

## 6. After Session

**Notarial Book Entry**: The system automatically creates your notarial book entry immediately upon session completion, compliant with Section 2, Rule VIII (A.M. No. 24-10-14-SC). Entries are populated in chronological order with all required details including entry number, date and time, document type and date, principals and witnesses with ID references, session mode, verification methods used, notarial fee, document control number, recording ID, and storage location. Access your notarial book at `/notarial-book` to view entries chronologically, search by date/document type/client name, and download exports.

**Session Recordings**: Sessions are recorded if enabled by you or the client, then stored securely per Supreme Court requirements. Recordings contain video of all participants, document presentation, signing process, and your verification procedures. Both you and the client maintain copies. Access recordings from `/sessions` by finding completed sessions and clicking "View Recording," then download or playback. Recordings remain accessible throughout the retention period.

**Payment**: You receive 90% of total fees while the platform receives 10%, automatically calculated and transferred. For example, on a ₱1,000 total, you receive ₱900 and the platform receives ₱100. Clients pay before the session starts, payment is confirmed in the system, the session completes, and your 90% share is credited to your account. For IEN sessions, clients may pay cash to you - click "Client Paid Cash" to confirm payment in the system. View your earnings at `/profile` → "Earnings" to see all payments and balances, request withdrawals, and download payment history.

**Analytics**: Track your performance including total sessions conducted, earnings summary, average client ratings, session mode breakdown, document types notarized, and Quick Match statistics. View analytics daily, weekly, or monthly to compare performance over time, identify busy periods, and track badge progress. Use this data to optimize your schedule, set better rates, improve client satisfaction, and grow your practice.

## 7. Quick Match System

**Scoring Algorithm**: Your ENP score determines how often you receive Quick Match requests. Rating Score (25%) reflects average client reviews weighted by recency. Speed Score (20%) tracks session duration vs expected and response time to booking requests. Experience Score (20%) counts total completed notarizations and years as ENP. Specialization Match (15%) matches your expertise with client document needs. Workload Balancer (20%) gives higher scores to ENPs with fewer recent sessions for fair distribution.

**Temporary Boosts**: New ENPs receive a +15% boost for the first 30 days. Returning-from-inactive ENPs get +10% when back after 30+ days. Rare specialization adds +10% for unique expertise few others have. These boosts help newer ENPs build their client base.

**Opt-In/Out**: Go to `/profile` → "Quick Match Preferences." Check "Opt-in to Quick Match" to start receiving requests, or uncheck to stop. No penalty for opting out. Set your preferences including maximum Quick Match sessions per day (default 5), session types you accept (notarization and/or consultation), modes (REN, IEN, Hybrid), and quiet hours when you don't want to receive requests.

**Managing Availability**: Go to `/profile` → "Availability" to add available time slots by selecting date ranges, setting start and end times, and repeating weekly/monthly if desired. Set unavailability by adding vacation days, blocking specific time slots, or adding recurring unavailability. Your availability affects when clients can book you, when you receive Quick Match requests, and your appearance in search results.

**Best Practices**: Keep your calendar updated regularly and block times you're unavailable. Provide enough notice for unavailability and consider setting buffer time between sessions. Opt-out of Quick Match when you're on vacation, fully booked, conducting complex sessions requiring focus, or if you prefer only Browse & Select requests. Declining while opted-in affects your score, so opt-out instead when unavailable.

## 8. Building Your Reputation

**Badge Categories**: Quality badges (Top Rated, Fast Responder) demonstrate service excellence. Experience badges (100+ Sessions, 500+ Sessions, 1000+ Sessions) show your expertise. Expertise badges (Specialist: Real Estate, etc.) highlight your specializations. Newcomer badges (Rising Star) help new ENPs get started. Verification badges (SC Accredited) are automatic for all verified ENPs.

**Earning Badges**: Top Rated requires a 4.8+ average rating with minimum 50 reviews, reviewed monthly. Fast Responder requires accepting 90%+ of requests within time limits with average response under 30 seconds, reviewed weekly. Session milestones (100+, 500+, 1000+ sessions) are permanent once earned. Specialist badges require 50+ sessions in a specific document category, self-declared specialization verified by session history. Rising Star applies to new ENPs in their first 30 days with a 4.5+ rating in first 10 sessions. SC Accredited is automatic for all verified ENPs.

**Rating System**: Clients rate you on professionalism, timeliness, communication, and overall satisfaction. You rate clients on preparedness, communication, punctuality, and payment reliability. Your client ratings affect your ENP score and Quick Match matching. Client reliability scores are shown to you, helping you make informed decisions. Both ratings build reputation on the platform.

**Trust-Building**: Complete your profile with a professional photo, detailed qualifications, clear specializations, and competitive rates. Deliver excellent service by starting sessions on time, being responsive and professional, explaining processes clearly, and handling issues gracefully. Earn badges by aiming for Top Rated and Fast Responder, building session milestones, and highlighting specializations. Maintain high ratings with a 4.5+ average target, responding quickly to requests, and accepting bookings you can fulfill. Use Quick Match effectively by setting realistic availability, opting-out when unavailable, and building your initial client base.

## 9. FAQ

**How do I set my rates?** Go to `/profile` → "Fees." Set your consultation rate (per 30 minutes) and notarization fees by document type. You can use platform defaults or customize based on your market and experience.

**What's the payment split?** You receive 90% of total fees while the platform receives 10%, automatically calculated and transferred. For example, on a ₱1,000 total, you receive ₱900 and the platform receives ₱100.

**How long are recordings stored?** Recordings are stored per Supreme Court requirements in secure, encrypted storage, accessible to you throughout the retention period. Contact support for the specific retention timeline.

**Can I conduct sessions abroad?** No, per SC ENF requirements. ENFs must prevent ENPs from using the system outside the Philippines. The only exception is limited extraterritorial performance at Philippine embassy/consular offices.

**How does Quick Match scoring work?** Your score is based on rating (25%), speed (20%), experience (20%), specialization match (15%), and workload balancing (20%). Temporary boosts apply to new and returning ENPs. Declining while opted-in can reduce your score by 5-10% for excessive declines.

**How do I get paid?** Payments are automatically credited to your account after session completion. Access them from `/profile` → "Earnings" to view balances, request withdrawals, and download payment history. Set up your payment method (bank account) to enable withdrawals.

**What if a client doesn't show up?** Mark the session as "No-Show" which affects the client's reliability score. You may charge a cancellation fee per your policy profile setting. Contact the client to reschedule if appropriate.

**Can I reject client requests?** Yes, for Browse & Select requests you can reject anytime. For Quick Match, declining affects your score if done excessively. Better to opt-out if you're unavailable. Providing a reason is optional but helpful.

## 10. Best Practices & Troubleshooting

**Before Sessions**: Review documents thoroughly, respond quickly to booking requests, keep your calendar updated, test your equipment regularly, prepare your workspace for IEN, communicate clearly with clients, set realistic availability, and opt-out of Quick Match when unavailable. Don't accept bookings you can't fulfill, leave requests unanswered, forget to review documents, have poor lighting for video calls, or overbook your schedule.

**During Sessions**: Start on time, verify all participants' identities, guide the process clearly, explain legal concepts when needed, be patient and professional, affix the notarial seal properly, ensure all documents are signed, and address client concerns promptly. Don't rush the process, skip verification steps, leave clients confused, be dismissive of questions, forget to notarize documents, or end sessions early without completion.

**After Sessions**: Ensure the notarial book entry is complete, verify payment received, follow up with clients if needed, leave professional responses to feedback, maintain good records, and update availability if needed. Don't forget to collect documents, neglect poor ratings, leave sessions unrecorded in the notarial book, or ignore client follow-ups.

**Common Issues**: If Quick Match isn't working, check if you're opted-in, verify your availability calendar, ensure your ENP score isn't too low, and check your internet connection. For payment issues, verify your payment method is set up, check your earnings balance, confirm bank account details, and contact support if needed. Liveness check failures usually require better lighting, asking participants to face the camera directly, removing face coverings, and allowing retries. Document upload issues often stem from file size problems (use PDF), incomplete documents, missing document type tags, or unset fees. Can't affix notarial seal? Verify your digital signature is set up, check that all parties have signed, ensure documents aren't locked incorrectly, and contact support if persistent.

**Support Channels**: Contact ENP Support for notary-specific issues, Technical Support for platform problems, Legal Resources for SC rules and compliance guidance, or the Community Forum to connect with other ENPs. Reach out for technical platform issues, payment or earnings problems, notarial book synchronization issues, SC integration issues, or digital signature problems. Contact the Supreme Court directly for questions about notarial rules, commission issues, compliance concerns, or e-notarization guidelines.
