### General Features of ENF

| ID     | Feature                                                            | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| :----- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **a**  | **Performance of IEN and/or REN**                                  | For every notarial act as defined in the Rules.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **b**  | **Electronic Know-Your-Customer (e-KYC)**                          | Consistent with the pertinent issuances of the BSP.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **c**  | **Multi-Factor Authentication Mechanism**                          | Consistent with the pertinent issuances of the BSP including:<br>1. Facial Recognition<br>2. Biometrics<br>3. One Time Password (OTP)<br>4. Others                                                                                      |
| **d**  | **Liveness Detection and Anti-Spoofing Technology**                | _No specific description provided in source._                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **e**  | **Electronic Signatures and Electronic Notarial Seal**             | Allows the creation and affixing of:<br>1. Electronic Signature of ENPs / Electronic Notarial Seal of ENPs / Notarial Certificate<br>2. Electronic Signatures of Principals and Witnesses                                               |
| **f**  | **Secure Document Handling**                                       | Before, during, and after the notarial act:<br>1. Secure Uploading<br>2. Secure Downloading<br>3. Secure Viewing<br>4. Secure Transmission                                                                                              |
| **g**  | **Simultaneous and Real time Collaboration of Parties**            | 1. Viewing of electronic documents<br>2. Affixing of electronic signatures and electronic seal                                                                                                                                          |
| **h**  | **Communication Accuracy of REN**                                  | 1. [Implied Audio quality]<br>2. Minimum of 1280x720 pixels (HD) videoconferencing system<br>3. Informs users of the 2 Mbps minimum required internet speed                                                                             |
| **i**  | **Geolocation Functionality**                                      | Identifying and disabling the use of VPNs or similar technologies that alter user location.                                                                                                                                             |
| **j**  | **Geo-restrictions**                                               | Prevents ENP from using the ENF to perform e-notarial acts outside the Philippines, except limited extraterritorial performance thereof.                                                                                                |
| **k**  | **Access to Audiovisual Recordings of REN**                        | Allows principal(s) to create and download audiovisual recordings of REN. Instructions should be included in the instructional video(s).                                                                                                |
| **l**  | **Recording Prompt**                                               | Notifies the participants when REN will be recorded.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **m**  | **Data Privacy Notice Prompt**                                     | Notifies users that personal information will be collected and processed in accordance with R.A. No. 10173 ("Data Privacy Act of 2012") and shall be shared with S.C. in accordance with e-Notarization Data Sharing Guidelines.        |
| **n**  | **Creation and Maintenance of Notarial Book**                      | 1. Automatically populated in chronological order<br>2. Properly referenced<br>3. Indexed by ENF Provider immediately upon completion of notarial act with required details under Section 2, Rule VIII                                  |
| **o**  | **Storage and Retention**                                          | Facility for storage and retention of notarized documents, Notarial books, and audiovisual recordings of REN.                                                                                                                           |
| **p**  | **Supreme Court Central Notarial Database Integration Capability** | 1. For automatic transmission of electronically notarized documents<br>2. For automatic submission or syncing of the Electronic Notarial Book<br>3. For verification of ENPs and ENF Provider credentials after the Transitional Period |
| **q**  | **Archival Format Compliance**                                     | Capability to convert or produce electronically notarized documents in PDF Archival Format or such format that is ISO Standards compliant and appropriate for long-time archiving.                                                      |
| **r**  | **Payment Facility**                                               | 1. Facilitates Digital Payments<br>2. Issues electronic Official Receipts                                                                                                                                                               |
| **s**  | **Tamper-Evident Technological Security Measures**                 | End-to-end encryption, as certified and declared acceptable by the Supreme Court.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **t**  | **Authenticity Verification Mechanism**                            | Mechanism to verify, authenticate, or validate whether an e-notarized document is authentic or tampered.                                                                                                                                |
| **u**  | **Access Management**                                              | 1. Restricts access for ENPs with expired, canceled, revoked commissions<br>2. Restricts access for Principals and Witnesses with expired or invalid IDs                                                                                |
| **v**  | **Real-Time Back-up Database**                                     | Maintains real-time back-up of all Electronic Notarial Books.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **w**  | **Data Privacy Compliance**                                        | Compliant with Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173); Registered with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).                                                                                                                              |
| **x**  | **Comprehensive Instructional Materials**                          | Provides instructional manuals and/or video(s) to applicants for commissioning as an Electronic Notary Public; Provides certification that the applicant has studied the materials.                                                     |
| **y**  | **System Uptime**                                                  | Guarantees 99.9% operational time except for maintenance operations, and provides advance notice to stakeholders of the schedule and duration.                                                                                          |
| **z**  | **Data Sharing**                                                   | Access of ENS and authorized officers of SC, subject to the provisions of the Electronic Notarization Data Sharing Guidelines, to information relating to ENP's commission and e-notarized documents.                                   |
| **aa** | **Supplementary Features**                                         | Provides other support facilities or features that may be required by ENS or SC.                                                                                                                                                        |
